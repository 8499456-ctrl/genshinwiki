Xingqiu Build Guide

Best weapons, Emblem artifacts, Energy Recharge targets, Hydro application teams, F2P options, and FAQ for Xingqiu.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: August 2026

Quick Answer

Best general Xingqiu build: 4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate, Sacrificial Sword or Favonius Sword, ER% or ATK% Sands, Hydro DMG Goblet, and CRIT Circlet. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge before chasing damage.

Xingqiu Overview

Xingqiu is a 4-star Hydro Sword character who provides off-field Hydro application, damage reduction, small healing, interruption resistance, and strong Burst damage. He is one of the most useful supports for Vaporize, Hyperbloom, Freeze, and many beginner-friendly teams.

Main roleOff-field Hydro applicator and Sub DPS.
Best artifact set4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate.
Best common weaponSacrificial Sword or Favonius Sword.
Talent priorityBurst > Skill. Normal Attack is ignored.

Xingqiu FAQ

Is Xingqiu worth building?

Yes. Xingqiu is one of the best 4-star characters because he adds Hydro application, defense, and off-field damage to many teams.

What is Xingqiu's best artifact set?

4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate is the best general set because it supports his Energy Recharge needs and increases Burst damage.

Does Xingqiu need Sacrificial Sword?

No, but Sacrificial Sword is very comfortable. Favonius Sword and other Energy Recharge weapons are also good.

Should Xingqiu use ER Sands or ATK Sands?

Use ER Sands if Burst uptime is not consistent. Use ATK Sands only when you already meet your Energy Recharge target.

Is Xingqiu better than Yelan?

They do different things. Xingqiu gives more defensive comfort and Hydro application, while Yelan gives more damage buffing. Many teams can use both.
