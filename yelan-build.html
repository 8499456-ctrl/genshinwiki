Yelan Build Guide

Best weapons, Emblem artifacts, HP and Energy Recharge targets, Hydro teams, F2P options, and FAQ for Yelan.

Quick Answer

Best general setup: 4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate is the best general build. Solve Energy Recharge first, then build HP, CRIT Rate, and CRIT DMG. Favonius Warbow is one of her best practical weapons.

Best Artifacts and Main Stats

  • 4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate: Best default because Yelan needs ER and deals Burst damage.
  • 2-piece HP / Hydro mixes: Usable if substats are much better.
  • HP Sands or ER Sands: Use ER Sands when Burst uptime is unstable; HP Sands when ER is solved.
Build rule: solve the stat that makes the character function first, then improve damage substats. A usable build today is better than perfect pieces weeks later.

Yelan FAQ

Is Yelan worth pulling?

Yes, especially if your account wants off-field Hydro damage. She is flexible and pairs well with many teams.

How much ER does Yelan need?

Solo Hydro Yelan often needs high ER, while Double Hydro or Favonius setups lower the requirement significantly.

Is Favonius Warbow good on Yelan?

Yes. It is one of her most practical weapons because it solves Energy Recharge and supports the team.

Does Yelan scale with ATK?

No. Yelan damage mainly scales with HP, so ATK stats are not useful for her damage.
