How To Read Damage Results

Average Damage

  • Use average damage when comparing CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG builds.
  • A lower CRIT hit can still be better if the CRIT Rate is much higher.
  • For Abyss planning, stable average damage is usually more useful than one perfect screenshot.

Common Inputs

  • Damage Bonus includes elemental bonus, normal attack bonus, burst bonus, or other relevant bonuses.
  • DEF Multiplier is often around 50% against same-level enemies without defense shred.
  • Enemy Resistance is commonly 10%, but shred effects can lower it.

Simple Damage Formula

Quick Formula

Estimated hit = scaling stat x talent multiplier x damage bonus x DEF factor x resistance factor x reaction factor.

Average damage then adds CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG with this simple factor: 1 + CRIT Rate x CRIT DMG.

What To Change First

  • If average damage is low, compare CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG first.
  • If both CRIT stats are already strong, test damage bonus and reaction multiplier.
  • If a support gives resistance shred or defense shred, update those inputs before judging artifacts.

Common Damage Scenarios

On-field DPS

For characters such as Arlecchino, Raiden, Mavuika, and Skirk, average damage is useful for comparing weapon and artifact upgrades. Do not judge a build only by its biggest CRIT hit.

Reaction DPS

For Vaporize and Melt hits, reaction multiplier can change the result dramatically. Use the reaction preset first, then adjust Elemental DMG Bonus, CRIT, and the correct talent multiplier.

HP / DEF Scaling

For HP or DEF scaling characters, enter the real scaling stat in the first field instead of ATK. The tool still works as long as the talent multiplier matches that stat.

Buff Comparison

To compare buffs, change one input at a time. For example, test the same build with 10% resistance, then with reduced resistance after a support effect.

Damage Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate Genshin damage?

Start with the scaling stat multiplied by the talent multiplier, then apply damage bonus, enemy defense, enemy resistance, CRIT, and reaction multiplier.

Is CRIT hit or average damage more useful?

Average damage is more useful for comparing builds because it includes CRIT Rate. CRIT hit is useful for checking your best possible single hit.

Does this replace a full simulator?

No. This tool is for quick planning. Full rotations, buffs, enemy behavior, ICD, and team timing still matter.
