How To Read Your Artifact Score

Score Meaning

  • S+ / S: lock the artifact. It is strong enough for serious builds or late-game optimization.
  • A: keep it. This is usually good for Abyss teams, especially on rare main stats.
  • B: usable for progression. Replace it later if you are farming a main DPS.
  • C / D: treat it as temporary unless it solves a very specific Energy Recharge or main stat problem.

What The Tool Rewards

  • DPS builds value CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, ATK%, and enough Energy Recharge to keep rotations smooth.
  • Reaction builds care more about Elemental Mastery and Energy Recharge, especially for Bloom, Hyperbloom, Swirl, and Burgeon teams.
  • Support builds can score highly with Energy Recharge, HP%, or DEF% when those stats match the character kit.

Best Stats By Character Type

Main DPS

Characters like Neuvillette, Arlecchino, Mavuika, Skirk, Raiden, and many on-field carries usually want CRIT stats first. After that, check whether they need ATK%, HP%, DEF%, Energy Recharge, or Elemental Mastery based on their kit.

Support And Sub DPS

Characters like Furina, Xingqiu, Bennett, Kazuha, Nahida, Yelan, and Xiangling often need enough Energy Recharge before damage stats feel good. For reaction teams, Elemental Mastery can be more useful than extra CRIT.

Artifact Scorer FAQ

What is a good artifact score in Genshin Impact?

An S or A score is usually worth keeping for the selected role. B pieces can still be useful for new accounts, rare main stats, or temporary builds.

Should I keep an artifact with a wrong main stat?

Usually no. A wrong main stat is hard to fix with good substats. Keep it only if the piece is very rare or you need it temporarily.

Are CRIT stats always the best artifact substats?

No. CRIT is excellent for many DPS builds, but Energy Recharge, Elemental Mastery, HP%, and DEF% can be stronger for supports or scaling-specific characters.

When should I stop leveling an artifact?

Stop early if the main stat is wrong or if upgrades repeatedly roll into low-value substats for the role you selected.
