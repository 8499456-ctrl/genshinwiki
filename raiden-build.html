Raiden Shogun Build Guide

Choose between Burst DPS and Hyperbloom builds, then pick the right weapon, artifacts, Energy Recharge, and team for Raiden Shogun.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: July 2026

Quick Answer

Best general Burst DPS build: 4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate, The Catch or Engulfing Lightning, ER% or ATK% Sands, Electro DMG or ATK% Goblet, and CRIT Circlet. Hyperbloom Raiden is different: build level 90 and Elemental Mastery instead of CRIT.

Raiden Overview

Raiden Shogun is a 5-star Electro Polearm character who can battery the team, deal strong Burst damage on-field, or trigger Hyperbloom from off-field with her Skill. The biggest mistake is mixing her Burst DPS build and Hyperbloom build together.

Hyperbloom Raiden Build

Hyperbloom Raiden is built almost completely differently. Her Skill triggers Dendro Cores from off-field, and Hyperbloom damage scales with Elemental Mastery and character level, not CRIT or Talent levels.

Raiden FAQ

Is Raiden Shogun still worth pulling?

Yes, if you need a flexible Electro unit who can play Burst DPS, battery teammates, or trigger Hyperbloom. Her value depends on whether your account needs those roles.

What is Raiden's best F2P weapon?

The Catch is her best free Burst DPS weapon. For Hyperbloom, use an Elemental Mastery weapon such as Dragon's Bane or another EM polearm.

Should Raiden use ER Sands or ATK Sands?

Use ER Sands if you need Burst uptime or use Engulfing Lightning. Use ATK Sands if your ER is already enough and it gives better damage in your team.

Does Raiden need CRIT in Hyperbloom?

No. Hyperbloom Raiden should focus on level 90 and Elemental Mastery. CRIT and Burst Talent levels are for her Burst DPS build.

Is C2 Raiden required?

No. C0 Raiden is usable. C2 is a major Burst DPS upgrade, but it is not required for National, Hyperbloom, or general team utility.

Should I crown Raiden's Normal Attack?

Usually no. Her Burst uses separate multipliers, so prioritize Burst first, then Skill. Normal Attack is not important for most builds.
