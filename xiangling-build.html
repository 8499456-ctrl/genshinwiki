Xiangling Build Guide

Best weapons, Emblem artifacts, Energy Recharge targets, Vaporize teams, National core, F2P options, and FAQ for Xiangling.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: August 2026

Quick Answer

Best general Xiangling build: 4-piece Emblem of Severed Fate, The Catch, ER% or ATK% Sands, Pyro DMG Goblet, and CRIT Circlet. Get enough Energy Recharge first, then improve CRIT and Elemental Mastery for Vaporize teams.

Xiangling Overview

Xiangling is a 4-star Pyro Polearm character with one of the strongest off-field Bursts in Genshin Impact. She is famous because Pyronado keeps dealing Pyro damage while another character is on-field, which makes her excellent in Vaporize and National-style teams.

Energy Recharge and Stats

Xiangling's biggest problem is not damage potential. It is getting Pyronado back every rotation. Bennett, Raiden, Favonius weapons, and good Energy Recharge substats all make her much easier to play.

Xiangling FAQ

Is Xiangling worth building?

Yes. Xiangling is one of the best long-term 4-star characters because her Burst works off-field and scales well with team buffs.

What is Xiangling's best F2P weapon?

The Catch is the best free option for most Xiangling builds. It is worth farming because it helps both Energy Recharge and Burst damage.

Does Xiangling need Bennett?

She can work without Bennett, but Bennett makes her much better by giving ATK, healing, Pyro particles, and easier rotations.

Should Xiangling use ER Sands?

Use ER Sands if Pyronado is not ready every rotation. If Burst uptime is stable, ATK or EM Sands can increase damage in Vaporize teams.

Is Xiangling good for new players?

Yes, but she feels much stronger after you unlock enough Energy Recharge and pair her with Bennett or a good Hydro support.
