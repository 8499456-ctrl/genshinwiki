Nahida Build Guide

Best weapons, Deepwood Memories artifacts, Elemental Mastery targets, Dendro teams, F2P options, and FAQ for Nahida.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: August 2026

Quick Answer

Best general setup: 4-piece Deepwood Memories is the safest default. Build Elemental Mastery first, then add CRIT when her personal damage matters. Nahida is valuable because she applies Dendro reliably and boosts reaction teams.

Best Artifacts and Main Stats

  • 4-piece Deepwood Memories: Best default because it shreds Dendro RES for Nahida and team Dendro damage.
  • 4-piece Gilded Dreams: Use if another teammate already holds Deepwood.
  • 2-piece EM mixes: Good temporary setup while farming better pieces.
Build rule: solve the stat that makes the character function first, then improve damage substats. A usable build today is better than perfect pieces weeks later.

Nahida FAQ

Is Nahida worth pulling?

Yes. Nahida is one of the most account-changing Dendro supports because she enables Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, Aggravate, and Spread teams.

What is Nahida best artifact set?

4-piece Deepwood Memories is the safest default. Use Gilded Dreams only if another teammate already uses Deepwood.

How much EM does Nahida need?

Many builds aim for high EM first, often around 700-1000 before buffs, then improve CRIT and Dendro damage depending on team.

Does Nahida need Energy Recharge?

Usually not much. Her Burst is useful, but her Skill is the core of her kit. Add ER only if Burst uptime matters in your team.
