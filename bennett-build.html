Bennett Build Guide

Best weapons, Noblesse artifacts, Energy Recharge targets, healing, ATK buff support teams, F2P options, and FAQ for Bennett.

Quick Answer

Best general setup: 4-piece Noblesse Oblige is the easiest support build. Use a high Base ATK sword, enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation, and HP or Healing Bonus if you want stronger healing.

Bennett FAQ

Is Bennett still worth building?

Yes. Bennett remains one of the strongest supports for ATK-scaling characters because he gives healing, Pyro energy, and a huge ATK buff.

What sword is best for Bennett?

Use a high Base ATK sword first. Energy Recharge is also valuable if it helps him Burst every rotation.

Should Bennett use HP or ATK artifacts?

Artifact ATK does not increase his Burst buff. Use ER, HP, and Healing Bonus for support comfort.

Is C6 Bennett bad?

C6 can limit a few older physical or infusion teams, but many modern Pyro and reaction teams benefit or do not care. Activate it only if you understand your teams.
