Character Ascension Materials

Use this checklist before farming a new Genshin Impact character from level 1 to 90.

Every character needs five material groups

Elemental gemstones
Slivers, fragments, chunks, and gemstones matching the character's element.
Local specialty
168 regional items, usually collected on a 48-hour respawn cycle.
Normal enemy drops
Common, graded, and rare drops converted at the crafting bench.
Boss material
46 unique drops from the character's world or weekly boss.
Mora
420,000 Mora for character ascension, before level-up costs.

Efficient farming order

  1. Farm the boss first and spend original resin while collecting gemstones.
  2. Gather the local specialty across two or three reset cycles.
  3. Use the Adventurer Handbook to route normal enemy drops.
  4. Convert lower-tier drops and gemstones only after checking all planned characters.
  5. Reserve Mora and Hero's Wit for the final leveling session.

Level 80 or level 90?

Level 80/90 unlocks the final ascension and is usually enough for most supports. Level 90 gives the biggest value to transformative reaction users, HP/DEF-scaling characters, and on-field carries.
