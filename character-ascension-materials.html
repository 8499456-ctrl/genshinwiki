Character Ascension Materials

A level 1 to 90 checklist for every Genshin Impact character.

Five material groups

Elemental gemstones
Slivers, fragments, chunks, and gemstones matching the element.
Local specialty
168 regional items collected across reset cycles.
Normal enemy drops
Common, graded, and rare drops converted at the crafting bench.
Boss material
46 unique world-boss drops.
Mora
420,000 Mora before level-up costs.

Efficient farming order

  1. Farm the boss first while collecting gemstones.
  2. Gather the local specialty across two or three reset cycles.
  3. Use the Adventurer Handbook to route enemy drops.
  4. Reserve Mora and Hero's Wit for the final leveling session.

Level 80 or level 90?

Level 80/90 is usually enough for supports. Level 90 has the highest value for transformative reaction users, HP or DEF scalers, and on-field carries.
