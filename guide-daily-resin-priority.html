Daily Resin Priority: What to Farm Today

Use this order when you log in and do not know where to spend resin. It prevents resin from sitting at the cap and keeps your main team moving forward.

Quick Answer

Default priority: time-limited materials first, then the most important upgrade for your main character, then Mora or EXP books if they are blocking progress. Artifact farming is the flexible leftover option.

Daily Resin Checklist

1

Talent and weapon domains

Farm these when the correct domain is open and you need the material. Their schedule makes them more urgent than general resources.

2

Weekly boss claims

Do these early in the week if a main character needs the drop. Do not leave the needed boss material until the last day.

3

Character ascension boss

Use resin here when a character is stuck below their next useful level breakpoint.

4

Mora and EXP books

Farm leylines when they are the only thing preventing a weapon, level, or talent upgrade. Mora is never wasted.

5

Artifacts

Spend leftover resin on one efficient domain for a main team. Stop when upgrades are tiny or another character has a bigger need.

What to Farm on Different Days

Your SituationBest Resin Use
A new character has low talentsTalent domain on the right day, then boss materials.
Your carry is underleveledWeapon materials, character boss, Mora, and EXP books.
Your team cannot burst on timeArtifacts with Energy Recharge main/substats, plus key talent levels.
Your main team is playableArtifacts for the weakest slots or begin building team two.

Use Fragile Resin Carefully

Fragile Resin is best for a clear target, not for panic farming. Spend it when you know the exact build goal and the domain is available.

  • Good: finishing an important weapon or talent milestone.
  • Good: getting a new main character ready before an event or Abyss reset.
  • Less good: endless artifact runs without a planned stopping point.
