Genshin Impact Spiral Abyss Strategy Guide

A practical Abyss planning guide for choosing two teams, managing energy, improving rotations, and turning close clears into consistent full-star runs.

Abyss Planning Basics

  • Match the enemy layout: check whether each half needs single-target damage, area damage, crowd control, shield breaking, or mobility.
  • Protect your best core: do not split key partners unless both teams still function.
  • Energy first: build enough Energy Recharge and funnel particles during low-pressure moments.
  • Reset smart: reset early when chamber one goes badly instead of finishing a doomed run.

Team Selection Checklist

  • Many small enemies: bring grouping, wide area damage, and fast elemental application.
  • Boss chamber: bring strong single-target damage and reliable uptime.
  • Elemental shields: bring the correct counter element and fast application.
  • High incoming damage: bring healing, shielding, interruption resistance, or defensive rotations.

Rotation Improvement

  1. Start with buffs and resistance shred before the main damage window.
  2. Use support skills before bursts when they generate particles.
  3. Funnel energy to burst-reliant characters by swapping into them as particles arrive.
  4. Save important bursts for the next wave when current enemies are almost defeated.
