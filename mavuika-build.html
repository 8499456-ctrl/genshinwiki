Mavuika Build Guide

Best weapons, artifacts, Pyro DPS teams, Nightsoul planning, F2P options, rotations, and FAQ for Mavuika.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: August 2026

Quick Answer

Best general Mavuika build: build her as a Pyro damage dealer first. Prioritize the artifact set that best supports her Nightsoul playstyle, then balance CRIT, Pyro DMG, ATK, and enough Energy or rotation comfort for your team.

Mavuika Overview

Mavuika is the Pyro Archon of Natlan and a high-interest character for players searching for Pyro carry teams, Natlan mechanics, and premium pull value. The main goal is simple: keep her damage window supported by the right buffs, Hydro or Cryo reactions when needed, and a team that does not break her rotation.

Mavuika FAQ

Is Mavuika worth pulling?

She is worth considering if your account needs a premium Pyro damage dealer or Natlan-centered team core. If you already have several built Pyro carries, compare her team flexibility before spending wishes.

Does Mavuika need Xilonen?

No, but Xilonen is a strong partner because she can improve team damage and comfort in many Natlan-era teams. Mavuika can still work with Bennett, Kazuha, Yelan, Furina, Xingqiu, or Zhongli.

Should Mavuika use Vaporize teams?

Vaporize teams are a strong default when Hydro application fits her rotation. If reactions feel inconsistent, a buff-focused Pyro or Natlan core can be easier to play.

What should F2P players prioritize?

Secure good main stats, enough rotation comfort, and a stable team before chasing premium weapons or constellations.
