Skirk Build Guide

Best weapons, artifact stats, Cryo DPS teams, Freeze options, F2P choices, rotations, and FAQ for Skirk.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: August 2026

Quick Answer

Best general Skirk build: treat her as a Cryo on-field damage dealer. Start with CRIT, Cryo DMG, ATK, and enough Energy for your rotation, then choose Freeze, Mono-Cryo, or buff-heavy teams depending on your account.

Skirk Overview

Skirk is a high-interest Cryo character because many players search for her after seeing Tartaglia story connections and official character media. For practical building, focus less on lore and more on whether your team can keep her damage window buffed and protected.

Skirk FAQ

Is Skirk worth pulling?

She is worth considering if you want a Cryo on-field carry and already have supports that can buff, protect, or enable her damage window.

Does Skirk need Freeze teams?

No. Freeze is comfortable when enemies can be Frozen, but boss chambers often require Mono-Cryo or buff-heavy teams instead.

What stats should Skirk prioritize?

Start with CRIT, Cryo DMG, and ATK. Add Energy Recharge if your chosen rotation depends on Burst uptime.

Is Skirk F2P friendly?

She can be built without premium weapons, but F2P players should prioritize a stable team and good artifact main stats first.
