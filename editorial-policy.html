Editorial Policy

GenshinWiki.cn is an independent fan-made guide site created and maintained by Song. This page explains how we make the guides useful, honest, and easy to correct.

Our standard: every guide should answer a real player question with specific reasoning, practical steps, and enough context for a reader to make their own decision. We do not treat search keywords or a list of facts as a finished article.

Who writes and edits the site?

Song is the site owner and primary editor. The author name shown on the blog identifies the person responsible for the article, while the site owner is responsible for the final wording, links, and update date. We use “GenshinWiki Editorial” only for reference pages that combine data tables with editorial notes.

How we create an original guide

  1. Start with a player problem, such as choosing between two weapons or planning resin for a new character.
  2. Write the recommendation in our own words and explain the decision criteria, trade-offs, and situations where the recommendation changes.
  3. Check game names, costs, mechanics, and version-sensitive details against in-game observations or official HoYoverse announcements when available.
  4. Add practical examples, limitations, and alternatives instead of repeating a generic tier list.
  5. Re-read the page after publication and update or label information that can change with a patch.

Sources and uncertainty

Official announcements are preferred for release dates, event rules, and announcements. For build advice, we combine in-game testing, gameplay observations, and comparison of commonly used team rotations. Leak, prediction, and theorycraft information is labeled as such; it is never presented as confirmed fact.

Corrections and updates

Patch-sensitive pages show a version or update date where practical. If you find an incorrect value, broken link, or outdated recommendation, email st.sqa@163.com with the page URL and a short explanation. We review corrections and update the page when the evidence supports a change.

Advertising and independence

Some pages may display advertising or affiliate links. Advertising does not determine our recommendations or editorial decisions. GenshinWiki.cn is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by HoYoverse, miHoYo, or Genshin Impact.
