Xilonen Build Guide

Best support weapons, Scroll artifacts, Energy Recharge, team buffs, Geo support teams, F2P options, and FAQ for Xilonen.

Quick Answer

Best general Xilonen build: use her as a support first. Prioritize the Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City style support set, enough Energy Recharge, and weapon choices that improve team uptime, buffs, or comfort.

Xilonen Overview

Xilonen is a Geo support from Natlan whose value comes from improving team damage and making rotations smoother. Players usually search her build because they want to know whether she replaces Kazuha, works with Furina, or supports newer Natlan carries like Mavuika.

Xilonen FAQ

Is Xilonen worth building?

Yes, if your account has carries that benefit from strong support slots. She is especially valuable when her team utility improves the whole rotation.

Is Xilonen better than Kazuha?

They are not the same. Kazuha brings grouping and Anemo utility, while Xilonen is a Geo support with different buff and team-building value. Many accounts want both.

Does Xilonen need her signature weapon?

No. The signature is premium value, but Favonius or Energy Recharge options can be better for comfort and team consistency.

What is Xilonen's best artifact set?

Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City is the safest support direction for most teams. Use mixed sets only while farming better pieces.
