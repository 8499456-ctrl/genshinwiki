Zhongli Build Guide

Best shield support weapons, Tenacity and Petra artifact choices, HP targets, team comps, F2P options, and FAQ for Zhongli.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: August 2026

Quick Answer

Best general Zhongli build: 4-piece Tenacity of the Millelith, Black Tassel or Favonius Lance, HP% Sands, HP% Goblet, and HP% Circlet. This gives a strong shield, easy play, and useful team buffs.

Zhongli Overview

Zhongli is a 5-star Geo Polearm character built mainly as a universal shield support. His value is simple: he makes teams safer, lowers enemy resistance through his shield, and lets damage dealers finish combos without being interrupted.

Zhongli FAQ

Is Zhongli still worth building?

Yes. Zhongli is not always the highest damage option, but he is one of the best comfort supports in the game because his shield protects rotations and reduces enemy resistance.

How much HP should Zhongli have?

For shield support, many players aim for roughly 40,000 HP or more. More HP gives more comfort, but do not ignore team needs such as Favonius energy.

Is Black Tassel better than Favonius Lance?

Black Tassel gives a stronger shield. Favonius Lance gives more team energy. Choose Black Tassel for comfort and Favonius for teams that need smoother Bursts.

Should Zhongli use HP or Geo damage?

Use HP for shield support. Geo damage is only for hybrid or Burst-focused builds, which are usually less efficient for most accounts.

Does Zhongli need constellations?

No. C0 Zhongli already gives the main value most teams want: a strong shield and resistance shred.
