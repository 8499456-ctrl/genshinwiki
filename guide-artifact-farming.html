Genshin Impact Artifact Farming Guide

Build a usable set first. Save your resin for upgrades that make a real difference instead of chasing a perfect piece too early.

Quick Answer

Farm artifacts seriously after AR 45. Before that, level weapons, characters, and key talents. At AR 45 and beyond, start with correct main stats, then improve substats only for characters in your main teams.

What to Keep First

Correct main stats normally beat fancy substats. A basic artifact with the right stat lets a character do their job today.

  • Damage dealer: ATK%, Elemental DMG Bonus, CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG.
  • Reaction character: Elemental Mastery on the pieces that matter.
  • Energy-hungry support: Energy Recharge first, then their useful scaling stat.
  • Healer or shielder: HP%, DEF%, Healing Bonus, or Energy Recharge depending on the kit.
Keep flexible pieces with good main stats even if the set is not perfect. They can save you days of farming later.

How Long Should You Farm One Domain?

Stop after you reach a basic team target: correct main stats on the important slots, enough Energy Recharge for rotations, and a couple of decent substats. Move to weapon levels or talents when the next artifact upgrade is only a tiny improvement.

  1. Get the right main stats.
  2. Make the rotation work without energy problems.
  3. Upgrade only the best pieces to +16 or +20.
  4. Return later when your other team members are built.
