Arlecchino Build Guide

Best weapons, artifacts, main stats, teams, F2P options, and FAQ for building Arlecchino as a Pyro on-field DPS.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: July 2026

Quick Answer

Best general build: 4-piece Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy, ATK% or EM Sands, Pyro DMG Goblet, CRIT Circlet, and a CRIT polearm. Prioritize Normal Attack first, then Skill, with Burst mainly used for emergency healing.

Arlecchino Overview

Arlecchino is a 5-star Pyro Polearm character who plays as an on-field damage dealer. Her damage comes from empowered Pyro Normal Attacks and her Bond of Life mechanic, so she wants strong offensive stats, stable buffs, and careful defensive support.

RolePyro Main DPS / on-field carry
Best teamsVaporize, Melt, Overload, and Mono Pyro
F2P optionWhite Tassel with Bennett or other ATK support
Talent priorityNormal Attack > Skill > Burst

Best Artifacts and Main Stats

Recommended artifact sets

  • 4-piece Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy: best general target because it directly supports her Bond of Life gameplay.
  • 4-piece Gladiator's Finale: easy starter set if you already have strong pieces with good CRIT stats.
  • Mixed 2-piece sets: acceptable while farming, but replace them once you get a strong 4-piece set.

Arlecchino FAQ

Is Arlecchino worth pulling?

Yes, if you want a powerful Pyro on-field DPS and enjoy active Normal Attack gameplay. She is less ideal if you mainly want an off-field support.

What is Arlecchino's best weapon?

Crimson Moon's Semblance is her best dedicated weapon. For non-signature options, Primordial Jade Winged-Spear and Staff of Homa are strong 5-star choices, while White Tassel is the easiest F2P pick.

Can Arlecchino work without Bennett?

Yes, but Bennett is one of her strongest supports because he solves much of her ATK need. Without Bennett, prioritize stronger artifact substats, better weapons, or team buffs from other supports.

Which artifact set should I farm for Arlecchino?

Farm Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy if you want her long-term best general set. Use Gladiator's Finale first if your Gladiator pieces already have better stats.

Does Arlecchino need Energy Recharge?

Usually not much. Her Burst is more of a comfort and emergency healing button, so offensive stats are normally more valuable unless your team plan relies on frequent Burst use.

Is C1 or C2 required?

No. Arlecchino is useful at C0. Constellations can improve comfort or damage, but good weapons, artifacts, and team choices matter first.
