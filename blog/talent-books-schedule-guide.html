Genshin Impact Talent Book Schedule

Talent book domains rotate on a fixed weekday schedule. Planning your resin around the correct day prevents wasted runs when preparing a new character.

Weekly schedule

DaysWhat to farm
Monday / ThursdayFreedom, Resistance, Prosperity, Transience, Equity, Contention
Tuesday / FridayBallad, Diligence, Gold, Elegance, Justice, Kindling
Wednesday / SaturdayPoetry, Light, Guide, Order, Conflict
SundayAll talent book domains are open

How many books for level 10?

One character talent from level 1 to 10 needs 9 green, 63 blue and 114 purple-equivalent books, plus Mora and weekly boss drops. Triple-crowning requires three times this amount.

Efficient farming order

  1. Confirm the character’s book family before spending resin.
  2. Farm the required weekly boss drop in parallel.
  3. Use crafting to convert lower-rarity books instead of over-farming.
  4. Reserve Sunday for flexible catch-up farming.
