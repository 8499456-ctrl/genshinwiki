Genshin Impact Character Build Order

A simple resin priority for new characters. Build a useful team member first, then decide if the character deserves more investment.

Quick Answer

Best default order: weapon level, character ascension, important talents, usable artifacts, then optional level 90 and deeper artifact farming. This gets most characters playable sooner than farming perfect artifact pieces first.

The 5-Step Build Order

1

Level the weapon first

Weapons give a reliable damage increase and usually cost less time than chasing artifacts. Get a main damage weapon to 80 early; push to 90 for characters who scale well with it.

2

Ascend the character to a useful breakpoint

For a new damage dealer, 80/90 is a strong first finish line. For many supports, 70/80 is enough until the rest of the team is stable.

3

Raise only the talents that matter

Do not level every talent automatically. Most characters have one or two talents that do most of their job. Aim for 6 first, then 8 or 9 on the important ones.

4

Use a good-enough artifact set

Correct main stats and enough Energy Recharge usually matter more than a perfect four-piece set. Borrow usable pieces from other characters before farming a new domain for days.

5

Decide whether to finish the build

Only push level 90, talent 10, and deep artifact farming when the character is part of your main Abyss team or a favorite you play often.

Off-field DPSLevel 80/90, burst/skill talent 8-9, enough Energy RechargeTheir damage is a major share of team damage.
Support / HealerLevel 70/80, key talent 6-8, correct Energy RechargeTheir scaling, healing, or reaction damage needs it.
Reaction scalerLevel 90 and correct main statsThey trigger the team's important reactions.

Do Not Farm Artifacts Too Early

Artifact domains can absorb unlimited resin. Before you settle into them, check these three things:

  • Your weapon is not underleveled.
  • Your important talents are not still at low levels.
  • You have enough Mora, EXP books, and boss materials for the next upgrade.
A character with a level 90 weapon, level 8 key talent, and average artifacts is usually more useful than a character with shiny artifacts but low talents and no Mora.
