Quick Answer

Best general setup: 4-piece Viridescent Venerer with EM / EM / EM is the best general support build. Kazuha wants enough Energy Recharge to Burst consistently, then as much Elemental Mastery as possible.

Best Artifacts and Main Stats

  • 4-piece Viridescent Venerer: Required for most teams because it shreds elemental resistance.
  • EM main stats: Use EM Sands, EM Goblet, and EM Circlet for support.
  • ER substats: Important if Burst is not ready every rotation.
Build rule: solve the stat that makes the character function first, then improve damage substats. A usable build today is better than perfect pieces weeks later.

Kaedehara Kazuha FAQ

Is Kazuha still worth pulling?

Yes. Kazuha remains one of the best general supports because he combines grouping, RES shred, elemental damage bonus, and easy team fit.

Should Kazuha use EM or CRIT?

Most support Kazuha builds use full EM. CRIT is mainly for Favonius activation or niche personal damage builds.

How much ER does Kazuha need?

Many teams like roughly 160%-200% ER depending on weapon, rotation, and particle generation.

Is Iron Sting good enough?

Yes. Iron Sting is a solid craftable F2P option if your artifacts provide enough Energy Recharge.
