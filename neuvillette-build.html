Neuvillette Build Guide

Best weapons, Marechaussee Hunter artifacts, HP vs Hydro stat choices, Furina teams, C1 value, and FAQ for Neuvillette.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: July 2026

Quick Answer

Best general build: 4-piece Marechaussee Hunter, HP% Sands, Hydro DMG or HP% Goblet, CRIT Circlet, and enough Energy Recharge to Burst comfortably. Neuvillette is strongest as an on-field Hydro DPS using enhanced Charged Attacks.

Neuvillette Overview

Neuvillette is a 5-star Hydro Catalyst character who deals most of his damage through enhanced Charged Attacks. He scales with HP, can self-sustain, and fits many teams, but C0 players often appreciate interruption resistance while charging.

RoleOn-field Hydro DPS / driver
Best artifact4-piece Marechaussee Hunter
Best partnerFurina is one of his strongest supports
Talent priorityNormal Attack > Level 90 > Burst = Skill

Best Artifacts and Main Stats

Recommended artifact sets

  • 4-piece Marechaussee Hunter: best general set because Neuvillette naturally changes HP and uses Charged Attacks.
  • 4-piece Heart of Depth: usable temporary set while farming Marechaussee Hunter.
  • 2-piece mixed sets: acceptable if the substats are much better. Use HP%, Hydro DMG, or Charged Attack-oriented pieces.

Energy Recharge and Rotation Comfort

Neuvillette wants enough Energy to Burst when the rotation needs more Sourcewater Droplets. His ER needs are lower with Prototype Amber, Furina, or other Hydro teammates, and higher in solo Hydro teams without Energy support.

Neuvillette FAQ

Is Neuvillette worth pulling?

Yes, if you want a powerful on-field Hydro DPS who is easy to pilot and strong across many teams. He is one of the most account-friendly carry options.

Is C1 Neuvillette worth it?

C1 is valuable because it improves comfort and team flexibility, especially by reducing interruption concerns and making Furina teams easier. C0 is still strong enough for most players.

What is Neuvillette's best artifact set?

4-piece Marechaussee Hunter is his best general set because he naturally changes HP and deals most damage through Charged Attacks.

Should Neuvillette use Hydro Goblet or HP Goblet?

Use the better piece. Hydro Goblet is often strong, but HP% Goblet can be competitive when it has better substats or when the team already gives a lot of damage bonus.

Does Neuvillette need Furina?

No, but Furina is one of his best supports. She adds damage bonus, Hydro resonance, off-field damage, and strong synergy with his HP-changing playstyle.

What is Neuvillette's best F2P weapon?

Prototype Amber is the safest free option because it gives HP and helps Energy comfort. It is easy to craft and works well in many teams.
