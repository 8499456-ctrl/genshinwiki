Furina Build Guide

Best weapons, Golden Troupe artifacts, Energy Recharge targets, healer pairings, team comps, F2P options, and FAQ for Furina.

Updated by Genshin Wiki Team · Last updated: July 2026

Quick Answer

Best general build: 4-piece Golden Troupe, HP% or ER Sands, HP% Goblet, CRIT Circlet, and enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation. Most Furina teams need a reliable healer because her Skill drains team HP while her Burst rewards HP changes.

Furina Overview

Furina is a 5-star Hydro Sword character who works as an off-field damage dealer and teamwide buffer. Her Skill provides long-lasting Hydro damage, while her Burst converts team HP changes into a large damage bonus for the party.

Energy Recharge Targets

Furina's Burst is usually the reason she is on the team, so missing Burst every rotation hurts both damage and comfort. Use these as practical starting targets:

Team SetupER TargetNotes
Solo Hydro200%+Use ER Sands or an ER weapon unless your team has strong battery support.
Double Hydro150%-210%Lower if the second Hydro generates many particles or uses Favonius.
With Neuvillette130%-150%Longer rotations and Hydro particles make this one of her easier teams to recharge.
Triple Hydro130%-140%Usually comfortable, letting Furina focus more on CRIT and HP.
Simple rule: If Furina does not Burst on time, add ER first. A lower-damage Furina who Bursts every rotation is usually better than a high-CRIT Furina who misses Burst.

Best Artifacts and Main Stats

Recommended artifact sets

  • 4-piece Golden Troupe: best general set because most of Furina's personal damage comes from her off-field Skill.
  • 2-piece mixes: usable while farming. Combine Golden Troupe, HP%, Hydro DMG, or Energy Recharge pieces if the substats are better.
  • Temporary support sets: only use them if your team specifically needs the utility and you accept lower Furina damage.

Furina FAQ

Is Furina worth pulling at C0?

Yes. Furina is already valuable at C0 because she brings off-field Hydro damage and a powerful teamwide buff. C2 is strong, but C0 is enough for most accounts.

Does Furina need a healer?

Most teams should run a healer with Furina. Her Skill drains team HP, and her Burst gains value from HP changes, so healing keeps her damage and buff active over multiple rotations.

How much Energy Recharge does Furina need?

Many teams need roughly 150%-210% ER, depending on Hydro teammates, Favonius weapons, and rotation length. Solo Hydro Furina often needs 200% or more.

What is Furina's best artifact set?

4-piece Golden Troupe is her best general set because it boosts the off-field Skill damage that makes up most of her personal damage.

Should Furina use HP Goblet or Hydro Goblet?

Use the better piece. HP% Goblet is often easier and very competitive, especially before C2, while Hydro DMG can win if its substats are better.

What is Furina's best F2P weapon?

Fleuve Cendre Ferryman is a strong free-friendly choice because it helps with Energy Recharge. Festering Desire is excellent if your account owns the old event weapon.
